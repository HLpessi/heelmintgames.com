The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13. Because the Game does not collect any personal information, no personal information is collected from any user, including children.

Because the Game does not collect personal data, we do not retain any user data on our servers. Your local save data can be removed at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data in your device settings.
