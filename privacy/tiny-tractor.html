Privacy Policy for Fat Fields: Harvest Sim

(previously released as Tiny Tractor: Farming Sim)

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Heelmint ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Fat Fields: Harvest Sim (the "Game").

We are based in Finland. By downloading or using the Game, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The Game itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All gameplay data (such as your progress, coins, upgrades, and settings) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The Android version of the Game uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display optional rewarded video advertisements (for example, to unlock the AUTO CUT and AUTO TAP helpers for a limited time). The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and processes certain information from your device in order to deliver, measure, and improve advertising. This information may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, country)
  • Information about your interactions with advertisements
  • Diagnostic and performance data

This data is collected directly by Google and is used for purposes such as serving relevant ads, measuring ad performance, fraud prevention, and analytics. We do not control how Google processes this data.

For more information, please see Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The web version of the Game does not include advertising or any other third-party services.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA/UK Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the legal basis for processing your data through Google AdMob is your consent (where required) or the legitimate interest of providing a free, ad-supported game.

When you first launch the Android version of the Game, you may be presented with a consent prompt allowing you to accept or decline personalized advertising. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the Game's settings menu ("Privacy options") or in your device settings as described below.

4. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access the data collected about you
  • The right to request correction or deletion of your data
  • The right to object to or restrict processing
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise these rights with respect to data collected by Google, please use the tools and contact details in Google's Privacy Policy. For any questions related to the Game itself, contact us using the details below.

5. Advertising ID and Opt-Out

You can reset or limit the use of your Advertising ID at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID, or Delete advertising ID

This will limit personalized advertising in the Game and other apps on your device. Advertisements in the Game are optional: the Game is fully playable without watching any ads.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Retention

Because the Game itself does not collect personal data, we do not retain any user data on our servers. Your local save data can be removed at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data in your device settings. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own retention policies.

8. Data Transfers

Google may transfer and process data outside of the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers are made under appropriate safeguards as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Security

We rely on Google's security measures for data collected through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google uses industry-standard practices, including encryption in transit (TLS), to protect data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
